Humans and their tools mutually define themselves and co-evolve over time....
We become what we build for ourselves. [1]

Entanglement HCI; Future-oriented design; LLM-based multi-agent social simulation

I believe the future is not merely something waiting to be understood; our expectations of the future can also shape reality. Therefore, I seek to design future-oriented interactive systems that help people reflect on what kind of future technologies we want, and who we hope to become through them.

About

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the College of Design and Innovation (D&I), Tongji University, Shanghai, China, under the supervision of Prof. Yang Shi. Previously, I received an M.E. in Mechanical Engineering from the College of Design and Innovation (D&I), Tongji University, under the supervision of Prof. Yunsheng Su, where I was awarded the National Scholarship for Graduate Students. I received a B.E. in Architecture from Zhengzhou University, where I was honored as an Outstanding Student of Henan Province.
